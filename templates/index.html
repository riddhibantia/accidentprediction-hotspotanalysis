Why XGBoost Outperforms Others?

Standard models like Logistic Regression struggle with non-linear feature interactions between spatial cluster IDs and weather conditions. Random Forest reduces variance via bagging, but XGBoost utilizes gradient boosting with regularized objective functions, capturing extreme risk boundaries and handling class imbalance effectively.
